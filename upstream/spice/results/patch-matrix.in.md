# spice-server patches: shaped-link measurements

Generated by `../rig/summarise-spice.py` from five runs of kerbside's
`tools/shaped-link/run-matrix.sh` on 2026-10-30. This preamble is
`patch-matrix.in.md`; everything after "Verdicts" is generated. The raw
run directories are not committed.

## Summary

Each verdict uses the rule given under "Verdicts" below: a difference
counts only when the repeats separate and the gap exceeds their spread.
The rule is not free of false positives. The null check, two builds that
differ only by patches expected to be inert under stock qemu with MJPEG,
flags 1 of its 15 judged cells, so an isolated single-measure verdict in
one cell is within what the rig produces by chance; a pattern across
cells is not.

| Change | Verdict | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| 0002, trace ring 8 -> 64 | no measurable effect | Stock qemu, MJPEG: 14 of 15 judged cells show nothing. The two exceptions are single-measure and at the noise rate: peak RSS -8 MiB (spread 6) at 20 ms / 50 Mbit busy, and p50 +5 ms (spread 4) idle, where no drawable is streamable so the ring is never consulted, and where the all-patches build (which contains 0002) shows nothing. Every build created the same streams in every cell (21 per repeat with `all`, 19 with `filter`, 36 at 80 ms / 5 Mbit heavy), so the ring overflow the patch addresses did not delay stream creation here. |
| 0003, MAX_FPS 30 -> 60 | no measurable effect | v2 qemu at max-refresh-rate 60. spice-server's log shows the patch working as written: busy60 streams are created at 60 fps instead of 30, and the periodic re-measure reads 60 on both builds. But latency, qemu CPU, bandwidth and display messages per second are unchanged in all 12 cells, both codecs. Delivered display messages stay at 4 to 12 per second for every build, so spice-server's rate control, not the ceiling, sets the frame rate on these links. No regression on the slow links either. The two C-mjpeg flags are peak RSS +8 and -9 MiB, opposite signs at the floor, with two repeats. |
| 0004, hardware encoders on any GPU | helps on CPU, costs memory (mixed) | vah264enc engaged on every H.264 stream, from spice-server's log: 12 of 12 pipelines for hwprobe and 12 of 12 for all in B, 18 of 18 for all in C-h264, no software fallbacks. qemu CPU is 0.13 cores against 0.15 to 0.16 in every B cell, measurably so in three of the four (0.02 to 0.03 cores, about 15%). At 80 ms / 10 Mbit heavy its smaller output (5.5 against 7.3 Mbit/s) also cut latency, p50 186 -> 107 ms and p95 1262 -> 275 ms; elsewhere latency is unchanged. qemu's peak RSS rises 56 to 64 MiB in every H.264 cell, the VA driver loaded into qemu. That cost is paid only once a GStreamer encoder is built: with MJPEG (A, C-mjpeg) the all-patches build shows no RSS change. Rate control here acts on Ryll's reports for H.264 streams it cannot decode, the same for both builds. |
| Upgrade 0.15.2 -> master (+ crash fix) | helps | Stock qemu, MJPEG: 9 of 15 judged cells help, 4 show nothing, 2 regress. qemu CPU falls 0.03 to 0.04 cores (about 20%) in five cells: 20 ms / 50 Mbit busy and heavy, and the `all` busy cells at 80 ms. With `streaming-video=all`, p50 falls 12 to 18 ms and p95 21 to 264 ms at 80 ms / 10 Mbit heavy, 80 ms / 5 Mbit and 150 ms / 2 Mbit busy. Display bandwidth with `all` drops to a third or less (20 ms / 50 Mbit heavy 34.4 -> 9.8 Mbit/s), with a third or fewer of the display messages; image quality was not measured, so whether that is leaner encoding or fewer frames is not settled here. The regressions are p50 +19 ms (spread 18) at 80 ms / 10 Mbit busy `filter`, and p95 +83 ms (spread 71) at 150 ms / 2 Mbit heavy `all`, a saturated cell whose p50 is 7.3 s. Idle shows nothing. |
| All patches against master + fix | follows 0004 | Under stock qemu and MJPEG, 12 of 15 cells show nothing, with two helps and one regression scattered at the noise rate. Under H.264 it repeats 0004: the CPU saving and the RSS cost. |

No qemu exited during any case. Six repeats failed, all at 150 ms /
2 Mbit heavy with `streaming-video=filter`, where the link is saturated
by lossless images and the first key press did not draw within the
probe's 15 s (the repeats that did run measured about 13.5 s); that cell
is not judged.

## What was measured

Every run puts Ryll behind kerbside-proxy on a shaped veth link
(netem delay plus rate, both directions), with a keydraw guest on
virtio-vga drawing an animated background and a key box. Latency is
key press to the key box being drawn (a DRAW_COPY), 40 presses per
case. Each run uses one qemu, one codec and one max-refresh-rate, and
interleaves its displays (one per spice-server build) inside every
pass, so every comparison below is between two displays of one run.
The activity starts 8 s after boot (`ACTIVITY_DELAY=8`), after Ryll
has connected, so builds without the connect-crash fix are not killed
by streams that exist at connect.

| Run | qemu | Codec (client preference) | max-refresh-rate | Displays | Links (RTT / rate) | Activities | Streaming | Repeats |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| A | stock 10.0.13 | MJPEG (Ryll default) | 30 (fixed timer) | sys, mfix, trace, all | 20/50, 80/10, 80/5, 150/2 | busy:30:3, heavy:30:8 | all, filter | 3 |
| A-idle | stock 10.0.13 | MJPEG | 30 | sys, mfix, trace, all | 80/10 | idle | all, filter | 3 |
| B | v2 series | H.264 (`--experimental-h264`) | 30 (default) | mfix, hwprobe, all | 20/50, 80/10 | busy:30:3, heavy:30:8 | all | 3 |
| C-h264 | v2 series | H.264 | 60 | mfix, fps60, all | 20/50, 80/10, 80/5 | busy60:60:3, busy:30:3 | all | 3 |
| C-mjpeg | v2 series | MJPEG | 60 | mfix, fps60, all | 20/50, 80/10, 80/5 | busy60:60:3, busy:30:3 | all | 2 |

Activities are `name:fps:noise_bits`: the guest redraws its
background at that rate with that many bits of noise per pixel.

Why two qemus. Stock qemu's non-GL SPICE path pushes damage on a fixed
30 ms timer, so no stream exceeds about 33 fps whatever spice-server's
ceiling, and it cuts damage into 32-pixel-wide columns, smaller than
the 128x128 that radeonsi's vah264enc accepts. Neither the frame-rate
patch nor the hardware encoder patch can show anything under it. The
v2 qemu series (kerbside-patches `upstream/qemu/series/v2/`) sends each
damaged region as one DRAW_COPY (one 640x480 stream in this guest) and
paces damage by `max-refresh-rate`, which runs B and C use.

Display names:

| Display | spice-server | Built SHA |
|---|---|---|
| sys | Debian 13 libspice-server1 0.15.2-1+b1 (system library) | - |
| mfix | master adeb620a04 + 0001 (connect-crash fix) | f0debfc1dc |
| trace | mfix + 0002 (trace ring 8 -> 64) | 94c6b0b07e |
| fps60 | mfix + 0003 (MAX_FPS 30 -> 60) | bce421c3f8 |
| hwprobe | mfix + 0004 (hardware encoders on any GPU, software fallback) | 570f602d3f |
| all | mfix + 0002 + 0003 + 0004 | 66454f3891 |

The base for every patch is mfix, master plus the connect-crash fix,
since the patches sit on that. Plain master is not a separate display:
with the activity delayed past connect it differs from mfix only in
the crash, which these runs never trigger.

In B and C qemu ran with spice-server's debug log on
(`G_MESSAGES_DEBUG=Spice`) for every display, so that the encoder each
stream got, and the frame rates spice-server estimated, are read from
spice-server's own log rather than assumed. A runs without it.

## Provenance

- Host: AMD Ryzen 7 8845HS (Radeon 780M, Phoenix3), 16 threads, 28 GiB,
  Debian 13, kernel 6.12.113+deb13-amd64, Mesa 25.0.7 (radeonsi VA-API),
  GStreamer 1.26. KVM. The host also ran a desktop session.
- Stock qemu: Debian qemu-system-x86 1:10.0.13+ds-0+deb13u1.
- v2 qemu: upstream qemu 30e8a06b64aa58a3990ba39cb5d09531e7d265e0 with
  `upstream/qemu/series/v2/v2-000{1,2,3,4}*.patch` applied by `git am`
  (built tree da7dc58636967ae832d2b96dc0bacbe21c745d51, reports
  11.0.50), configured `--target-list=x86_64-softmmu --enable-spice
  --enable-kvm --disable-docs` against `upstream/qemu/rig/sysroot`'s
  0.15.2 headers. SPICE is linked into the binary, which loads
  `libspice-server.so.1` dynamically, so `LD_LIBRARY_PATH` swaps the
  server under it exactly as under stock qemu's module;
  `/proc/<pid>/maps` confirmed the library each wrapper loaded.
- spice-server builds: `../rig/build-spice.sh` (debian:trixie, Debian's
  flags, `meson test` 25 of 25), base master
  adeb620a04c43620416c5067b8bbfd1e4cf5eef5, spice-common 492c22f444,
  spice-protocol 0.14.5. Patch sha256 prefixes: 0001 e223ced28f51,
  0002 4c6d63743266, 0003 fd124d13877f, 0004 0d050a78e8ce. 0002 and
  0003 are kept unchanged in `../dropped/`. 0001 and 0004 became
  `../series/v1/`, with fuller commit messages and 0004 split in two;
  applied in order, v1 gives the same source tree as 0001 plus 0004.
- Ryll 0d140b3 plus kerbside's `tools/shaped-link/ryll-surface-drawn-rect.patch`.
  Ryll does not decode H.264 streams at this commit (ryll#398): in B and
  C-h264 the key box latency is valid, since the key box is a DRAW_COPY,
  but the video is not rendered, and the client's stream reports, which
  drive spice-server's rate control, describe streams it failed to
  decode.
- kerbside-proxy from kerbside dd4f56f, release build, default features;
  run-matrix.sh, keydraw guest and mock control plane from the same tree.
